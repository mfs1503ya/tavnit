Privacy policy

The data controller is the owner named in the legal notice above. For any privacy question, write to menudaapp@proton.me.

  • Your email address, when you join the waitlist.
  • Where you signed up from: which form on the page you used and, if you arrived through one of our links, the campaign tags in that link (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content; for example, which video brought you here). They are sent with your email to our email provider.

  • Email delivery: Kit (ConvertKit LLC), USA. Transfers outside the EU rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses in Kit's data processing agreement and on Kit's EU–US Data Privacy Framework certification.

  • Analytics (cookieless): Cloudflare Web Analytics. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used (GDPR art. 6.1.f).

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. After that we keep only what the law requires, such as a record of your unsubscribe so we do not email you again.
